# Deepwick

A turn-based dungeon crawler in pixel art, in one HTML file, built by a single prompt on Codex (GPT-6.1 Sol).

Play it: https://gradedprompts.github.io/deepwick/

`index.html` is the unedited output of one run of the prompt. Nothing was changed by hand after the run.
SHA-256: `636d81a9547a9d41711a8aa80e77d263e2d0fc588c7d2414e987e064d1bfca40`

How it plays: carry a lantern down eight floors of a dark dungeon. Only what the lantern lights can be seen, and its oil runs down. Monsters show on the map what they will do next turn: a fire beetle glows before it bursts, a skeleton archer marks its line, an ogre raises its club over your tile. Every turn is a small puzzle you can read. Walk into a monster to attack it, pick up potions, bombs, oil, blades and shields, and level up on the way down. The Moth Queen waits on the eighth floor; killing her wins.

Arrow keys or WASD to move (or swipe), tap or click a tile to walk there, Space to wait, Q for a potion, E for a bomb, Enter to take the stairs, P to pause, M for sound. It works on a phone in either orientation, needs no connection, and keeps your best run in your browser. Add `?floor=8` to the address to go straight to the Moth Queen.

The prompt that built it is being prepared for Graded Prompts: https://www.gradedprompts.com/prompts/apps
